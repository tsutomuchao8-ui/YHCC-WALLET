YHCC WALLET 2.0 patch

Copy the app/ directory over the existing project's app/ directory.

Features:
- Black/gold Android trading-terminal style
- Public CoinGecko market price and 7-day sparkline data, refreshes about every 60 seconds
- Local editable asset quantities and valuation prices
- Buy/sell order preview screen (does not submit orders)
- QR scan to import text into a transfer draft
- QR generation for a user-entered public address

Important:
- This is not a custody wallet or exchange.
- It does not generate private keys or blockchain addresses.
- It does not sign or broadcast blockchain transactions.
- It does not execute orders or move funds.
- Editable holdings are local records, not verified on-chain balances.
- Public market data may be delayed, unavailable, or rate-limited.
